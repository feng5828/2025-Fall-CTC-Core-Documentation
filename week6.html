Description
This project is created using generative codes and photoshop. I used the javascript to control and randomize the preset actions of photoshop and created the 16 images in the back as the 16 pages of the book. After this, I used the method of randomized codes recursively by applying them together to the collectioin of the 16 images. The visual aspect of this work as well as the recursive process are something that I want to explore more in the future.
